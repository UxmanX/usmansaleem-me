From Plant-Floor Commissioning to National Key Accounts & Custom Automation

I am a Mechanical Engineer (MEng) and Key Accounts Project Coordinator based in Mississauga, Ontario. My work sits right where physical engineering meets practical digital execution—reading architectural drawings, reviewing technician field take-offs (OFW, ROW, ROH), configuring commercial entrance packages, and building custom software tools that eliminate hours of repetitive administrative work.

At ASSA ABLOY Entrance Systems, I coordinate up to 40 concurrent modernization and replacement projects across major national accounts including Loblaw Companies, Shoppers Drug Mart, Costco Wholesale, The Home Depot, Staples, Sobeys, and BGIS/BMO. From specifying Besam SL500/SW200 operators, modular retrofit kits, and 14ft structural transoms to enforcing site readiness checklists, executing Change Orders, and running CAPEX/Planned Maintenance lifecycle studies (#0–#3 door condition ratings), I manage the full project path from quote to invoice.

Before ASSA ABLOY, I trained on the plant floor with Bühler Group—coordinating ~60 technicians during the installation and commissioning of a complete flour-milling and pasta plant for Ismail Industries—worked in machinery reliability at Style Textile, led a 15-person high-volume campus team as an Assistant Manager at Starbucks while in Ottawa, and completed my MEng in Mechanical Engineering at the University of Ottawa (with A+/A grades in Engineering Design, Manufacturing System Analysis, Finite Element Analysis, Surfaces & Coatings, and 3D Machine Vision) after earning my B.Sc. in Mechanical Engineering from UET Lahore.
