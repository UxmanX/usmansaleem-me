Automatic sliding entrances like this one are my day job: replacements and modernizations for national retail and commercial accounts, from technician take-offs and drawings to quotes, site readiness and change orders.

Kiosk · Facility audit & estimating

Installation & Facility Insight Collector

A multi-threaded desktop platform that replaces a 3-hour manual service-portal crawl for a 15-door store. It untangles invoice ledgers, separates emergency calls from planned maintenance, pulls door conditions and take-off dimensions from technician notes, calculates replacement ROI and drafts review-ready proposals.

The race on the kiosk screen is a simulation at 3× speed, using these case-study times.

  • Python 3.12 & PySide6
  • PowerShell RunspacePool
  • SSRS & OpenXML

Shelf · National key accounts

Take-Off Replacement Lead Assistant & QBR Platform

Used across 16+ national retail and commercial accounts. It assembles replacement-lead packages from reports, surveys and field photos in 20–30 seconds, and builds an 8-slide executive QBR deck in about 7 seconds, with Excel workbooks and repeat-callback analytics.

The pipeline on the screen is a simulation at 3× speed, using the tool's typical times.

  • Dynamics 365
  • Baan IV ERP & SQL
  • PowerPoint & Outlook COM

Scale model · Plant commissioning

Flour-milling & pasta plant, Karachi

With Bühler Group, I supported installation, erection and commissioning of a multi-line food-processing plant: coordinating about 60 workers and technicians, checking work against drawings and PFDs, leveling machinery and tracing faults to their root cause.

The scale model is illustrative: its line shows a simplified flow, not the plant's process drawings.
